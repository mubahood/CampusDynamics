## Overview

The Performance Appraisal System is a structured, multi-stage process that evaluates every employee's performance over a defined appraisal period. Each appraisal moves through four sequential stages:

| Stage | Performed By | Status After Completion |
|-------|-------------|------------------------|
| 1. Self Appraisal | Employee (via Portal) | `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED` |
| 2. Supervisor Review | Line Supervisor (via Admin System) | `COMPLETED` |
| 3. HR Final Review | HR Officer (via Admin System) | `HR_REVIEWED` |
| 4. Reports & Conclusions | HR / Management | Printed / Exported |

> **Important:** Each stage must be fully completed before the next can begin. You cannot skip stages.

---

## Performance Classification Scale

All scores are expressed as a percentage (0 – 100 %). Final scores are classified as follows:

| Classification | Score Range | Meaning |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| **Exceptional** | 90 % – 100 % | Outstanding performance; consistently exceeds all targets |
| **Very Good** | 75 % – 89 % | Exceeds most targets; high quality of work |
| **Good** | 60 % – 74 % | Meets all targets; solid and reliable performance |
| **Fair** | 40 % – 59 % | Partially meets targets; improvement required |
| **Unsatisfactory** | Below 40 % | Does not meet targets; immediate action plan required |

## Stage 1 — Self Appraisal (Employee)

> **Video Tutorial:** [▶ Self Performance Appraisal — University Portal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bvH9gncDpUo&list=PLOR5hj0X3WPdEO7BVd0QlGZGjxlmvRPBq&index=18) *(Tutorial #18 — watch before starting)*
> Also watch [▶ Introduction to Performance Appraisal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lxee5QzvV1Q&list=PLOR5hj0X3WPdEO7BVd0QlGZGjxlmvRPBq&index=18) *(Tutorial #17)* if this is your first time.

**Who:** All employees included in an active appraisal session.
**Where:** Employee Portal → *My Appraisal* (menu item in the portal navigation).
**System Status:** `PENDING` → `EMPLOYEE_IN_PROGRESS` → `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED`

---

### Step 1.1 — Access Your Appraisal

1. Log in to the **Employee Portal** at `eportal.mru.ac.ug`.
2. From the left navigation, click **My Appraisal**.
3. Your active appraisal record will be listed. Click **Open / Continue**.

> If you do not see an appraisal listed, contact HR — you may not have been added to the current session yet.

---

### Step 1.2 — Preflight Checklist (First Visit Only)

On your very first visit, a **setup screen (Step 1)** appears before the appraisal form. You must complete this before continuing.

| Checklist Item | What It Means |
|---------------|---------------|
| I have read and understood the appraisal guidelines | Confirms awareness of the scoring criteria and process |
| I understand my sections will be initialized on proceed | Acknowledges that form sections are created when you click Proceed |
| I confirm the details shown are correct | Validates that your name, department, and period are accurate |

**How to proceed:**

1. Read all three checklist items carefully.
2. Tick all three checkboxes.
3. Click **Proceed to Appraisal →**.
4. The system initializes your form sections and redirects you to **Step 2 — the appraisal form**.

> After the first visit, the preflight screen is skipped automatically and you go directly to your form.

---

### Step 1.3 — Complete the Appraisal Form Sections

The form is divided into labelled sections. Use the **step tabs** at the top to navigate between them.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Section A** | Personal & employment details (auto-filled; read-only) |
| **Section B** | Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) — rate yourself against set targets |
| **Section C** | Competencies & Behavioural Indicators |
| **Section D** | Training & Development needs |
| **Section E** | Employee comments & general remarks |

**Scoring guidelines for Sections B and C:**

| Rating | Score |
|--------|-------|
| Exceptional | 5 |
| Very Good | 4 |
| Good | 3 |
| Fair | 2 |
| Unsatisfactory | 1 |

- Fill in every required field. Fields marked with a red asterisk (`*`) are mandatory.
- Use the **Save** button after completing each section — your progress is saved automatically but saving manually is good practice.
- You can return to any section using the step tabs without losing data.

---

### Step 1.4 — Submit Your Self Appraisal

1. Navigate to the **last step tab** (Review & Submit).
2. Review your entries across all sections.
3. Click **Submit Appraisal**.
4. A confirmation dialog will appear — click **Confirm Submit**.

> **Once submitted, you cannot edit your responses.** The record moves to `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED` and your supervisor is notified.

---

### Step 1 — Quick Reference Table

| Action | Where | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Open appraisal | Portal → My Appraisal | Must be in an active session |
| Complete preflight | Step 1 tab | One-time only; tick all 3 boxes |
| Fill KPI section | Step 2 → Section B | Rate each KPI honestly |
| Fill competencies | Step 2 → Section C | Behavioural ratings |
| Fill training needs | Step 2 → Section D | List any training required |
| Add comments | Step 2 → Section E | General remarks |
| Submit | Review & Submit tab | Irreversible — confirm before submitting |

## Stage 2 — Supervisor Review

> **Video Tutorial:** [▶ Supervisor Performance Appraisal — University Portal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z1-uhFa7NbE&list=PLOR5hj0X3WPdEO7BVd0QlGZGjxlmvRPBq&index=19) *(Tutorial #19 — watch before starting)*

**Who:** Line supervisors / department heads assigned to the employee.
**Where:** Admin System → *Appraisals* → *Appraisal Sessions* → open the session → employee record.
**System Status:** `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED` → `SUPERVISOR_IN_PROGRESS` → `COMPLETED`

---

### Step 2.1 — Open the Employee Record

1. Log in to the **Admin System** at `erp.mru.ac.ug`.
2. From the sidebar, navigate to **HR** → **Appraisals** → **Appraisal Sessions**.
3. Locate the current session and click **View Records**.
4. Find the employee whose status is `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED`.
5. Click **Open / Review** on that record.

> Only employees with status `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED` are ready for supervisor review.

---

### Step 2.2 — Review Employee Self-Assessment

The supervisor view shows the employee's self-scores alongside editable supervisor-score columns.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| KPI / Competency | The item being evaluated |
| Employee Score | Score the employee gave themselves |
| Supervisor Score | Your score as the supervisor (editable) |
| Supervisor Remarks | Free-text notes per item (optional but recommended) |

**Scoring guidelines are identical to Stage 1** (1 = Unsatisfactory … 5 = Exceptional).

---

### Step 2.3 — Enter Supervisor Scores

1. For each KPI in **Section B**, enter your score in the **Supervisor Score** column.
2. Add remarks where the employee's self-score differs significantly from your assessment.
3. Score each competency in **Section C** using the same scale.
4. Review the employee's **Section D** (training needs) — endorse or amend as needed.
5. Add your **supervisor overall comments** in the remarks field at the bottom.

---

### Step 2.4 — Calculate and Finalise

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Click **Calculate Score** | System computes the weighted final percentage |
| Review the computed score | Confirm it reflects your assessment |
| Click **Complete Review** | Status changes to `COMPLETED`; record moves to HR |

> If you need to return to a record after saving (before completing), status remains `SUPERVISOR_IN_PROGRESS`. You can continue editing at any time until you click **Complete Review**.

---

### Step 2 — Quick Reference Table

| Action | Where | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Find pending records | Session → View Records → filter `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED` | |
| Enter supervisor scores | Section B & C columns | Must score every item |
| Add remarks | Per-item remarks + overall comment box | Remarks are visible to HR |
| Calculate score | Calculate Score button | Auto-computed; review before completing |
| Complete review | Complete Review button | Sends record to HR |

## Stage 3 — HR Final Review

> **Video Tutorial:** [▶ HR Performance Appraisal — University Portal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zXe2NuDnLs0&list=PLOR5hj0X3WPdEO7BVd0QlGZGjxlmvRPBq&index=21) *(Tutorial #20 — watch before starting)*

**Who:** HR Officers / HR Manager.
**Where:** Admin System → *Appraisals* → *Appraisal Dashboard* or *Appraisal Sessions*.
**System Status:** `COMPLETED` → `HR_REVIEWED`

---

### Step 3.1 — Access Completed Records

1. Log in to the **Admin System**.
2. Navigate to **HR** → **Appraisals** → **Appraisal Dashboard**.
3. The dashboard shows a summary of all session records, grouped by status.
4. Click on the **Completed** count, or navigate to **Sessions** → **View Records** → filter by `COMPLETED`.

---

### Step 3.2 — HR Review Checklist

For each completed record, HR should verify the following before marking as reviewed:

| Check | What to Look For |
|-------|-----------------|
| Scores are complete | No blank supervisor scores in Sections B or C |
| Score discrepancy | Employee vs. supervisor gap > 2 points warrants a note |
| Classification is correct | Computed percentage maps to correct classification band |
| Remarks are present | Supervisor has added comments, especially for Fair/Unsatisfactory records |
| Training needs captured | Section D is filled where the employee requested development |
| No anomalies | No duplicate submissions or system errors |

---

### Step 3.3 — HR Remarks and Adjustments

1. Open the record by clicking **HR Review**.
2. In the **HR Comments** field, add your final observations.
3. If a score correction is warranted (e.g., data entry error by supervisor), document the reason in HR Comments before adjusting.
4. Confirm the **Final Classification** shown is accurate.

---

### Step 3.4 — Mark as HR Reviewed

1. Once satisfied with the record, click **Mark as HR Reviewed**.
2. Status changes to `HR_REVIEWED`.
3. The record is now locked and included in final session reports.

> HR can print an individual appraisal form at any stage by clicking **Print Appraisal** — this generates a formatted PDF-ready page.

---

### Step 3.5 — Batch Operations (Optional)

For large sessions, HR can:

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Filter by department | Use the **Department** dropdown in the session records grid |
| Filter by classification | Use the **Classification** filter |
| Export to Excel | Click **Export** → Excel from the session records toolbar |
| Bulk mark reviewed | Select multiple records (checkbox) → **Bulk HR Review** button |

---

### Step 3 — Quick Reference Table

| Action | Where | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| View completed records | Dashboard or Session → filter `COMPLETED` | |
| Check score discrepancies | Open record → compare employee/supervisor columns | Gap > 2 = flag |
| Add HR comments | HR Comments field | Required for Fair/Unsatisfactory |
| Mark HR reviewed | Mark as HR Reviewed button | Locks the record |
| Print individual appraisal | Print Appraisal button | Any stage |

## Stage 4 — Report Generation and Conclusions

> **Video Tutorial:** [▶ HR Performance Appraisal — University Portal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zXe2NuDnLs0&list=PLOR5hj0X3WPdEO7BVd0QlGZGjxlmvRPBq&index=21) *(Tutorial #20 — report generation is covered in the second half of this video)*

**Who:** HR Manager / Senior Management.
**Where:** Admin System → *Appraisals* → *Appraisal Reports* / *Session Report*.

---

### Step 4.1 — Individual Appraisal Print

To print a single employee's appraisal form:

1. Navigate to **Sessions** → **View Records**.
2. Open any record (any status from `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED` onwards).
3. Click **Print Appraisal**.
4. A formatted print page opens in a new tab with the university header, all scored sections, and a sign-off block.
5. Use the browser's **Print** function (`Ctrl + P`) or the **Print** button on the page.
6. Set paper size to **A4**, orientation **Portrait**, and enable **Background Graphics** in print settings for correct styling.

---

### Step 4.2 — Session Summary Report

The session report provides an executive overview of an entire appraisal session.

1. Navigate to **HR** → **Appraisals** → **Appraisal Reports**.
2. Select the session from the dropdown.
3. Click **Generate Report**.

The report contains the following sections:

| Report Section | Contents |
|---------------|---------|
| **Executive Summary** | Total employees, completion rate, average score, pending count |
| **Session Details** | Session name, period, department scope, created by |
| **Completion Overview** | Stacked bar chart (completed vs. pending vs. in-progress) |
| **Performance Distribution** | Classification breakdown chart (Exceptional → Unsatisfactory) |
| **Category Breakdown** | Statistics per staff category (academic, admin, etc.) |
| **Employee Roster** | Full list grouped by category with individual scores and classifications |
| **Outstanding Actions** | Employees still pending / not submitted (if any) |
| **Sign-off Block** | Three-column signature grid for HR, HOD, and VC |

---

### Step 4.3 — Reading the Session Report

**Completion Overview Chart:**

| Bar Colour | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Green | HR Reviewed or Completed |
| Blue | In Progress (employee or supervisor) |
| Amber | Pending (not yet started) |
| Red | Cancelled |

**Performance Distribution Chart:**

| Colour | Classification |
|--------|---------------|
| Dark Green | Exceptional (≥ 90 %) |
| Blue | Very Good (75 – 89 %) |
| Teal | Good (60 – 74 %) |
| Amber | Fair (40 – 59 %) |
| Red | Unsatisfactory (< 40 %) |

---

### Step 4.4 — Category Breakdown Table

The category table helps management compare performance across different employee groups.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Staff Category | e.g., Academic, Administrative, Technical |
| Total | Number of employees in that category |
| Completed | Records fully reviewed by HR |
| Avg Score | Mean final percentage for completed records |
| Highest | Best individual score in the category |
| Lowest | Lowest individual score in the category |
| Completion Rate | Completed ÷ Total × 100 % |

---

### Step 4.5 — Printing the Session Report

1. On the session report page, click **Print Report**.
2. The page is optimised for A4 print — charts and tables are formatted for paper.
3. In your browser print dialog:

| Setting | Recommended Value |
|---------|------------------|
| Paper size | A4 |
| Orientation | Portrait |
| Margins | Default (10 mm) |
| Background graphics | **Enabled** (required for colour charts) |
| Scale | 100 % (do not shrink to fit) |

---

### Step 4.6 — Conclusions and Actions After the Session

Once all records are `HR_REVIEWED`, management should conduct a **Post-Appraisal Meeting** and document actions in the following categories:

| Outcome | Action Required | Responsible |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| Exceptional (≥ 90 %) | Consider for promotion, commendation, or bonus | HR + HOD |
| Very Good (75 – 89 %) | Acknowledge; fast-track for growth opportunities | HR + HOD |
| Good (60 – 74 %) | Maintain engagement; note any development areas | HOD |
| Fair (40 – 59 %) | Develop a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) | HR + HOD |
| Unsatisfactory (< 40 %) | Formal PIP; disciplinary review if repeated | HR + Management |

---

### Step 4 — Quick Reference Table

| Action | Where | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|

| Generate session report | Appraisal Reports → Select session → Generate | All HR_REVIEWED records included |

```

  └─► EMPLOYEE_IN_PROGRESS   (employee opens and begins form)
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| I submitted my appraisal by mistake — can I undo it? | No. Once submitted, the record is locked. Contact HR to request a recall if there was a genuine error. |
| My appraisal is not showing in the portal. | You may not be included in the current session. Contact HR with your staff ID. |
| The Proceed button is greyed out on Step 1. | All three preflight checkboxes must be ticked before the button becomes active. |
| Can I save and return to the form later? | Yes. Your progress is saved automatically. Return via Portal → My Appraisal at any time before submitting. |
| The supervisor score differs a lot from my self-score — what happens? | HR reviews all significant gaps. Remarks from your supervisor explain the difference. Raise any concerns with your HOD or HR. |
| When is a session considered closed? | When all records in the session are `HR_REVIEWED` and management has signed off the session report. |
| Who can see my appraisal scores? | You, your assigned supervisor, and HR Officers/Managers. Scores are not visible to colleagues. |
