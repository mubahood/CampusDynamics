**Prepared:** 15 August 2026

## 1. How a student was counted

Three tests, applied in order:

1. **Entry year gives the year of study** — 2026 → Year 1, 2025 → Year 2, 2024 → Year 3.
2. **The programme must still be running for them.** A 2024 entrant on a three-year degree is in
   Year 3. A 2024 entrant on a two-year diploma finished last year and is not counted.
3. **Past Year 1, more than UGX 500,000 in positive transactions.** Any credit counts — payment,
   bursary or waiver.

Semester registration is not used.

## 10. Three things for Senate to act on

**1. Half of the diploma final-year students show no activity.**

| Diploma Year 2 (entry 2025) | Students | Papers sat 2025/26 | Average transactions |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Counted | 243 | 11.6 average | 2,446,815 |
| **Excluded** | **244** | **0.2 average — 225 sat nothing** | **35,111** |

A completion list built from records alone would carry all 487 names. This should be settled
before any diploma award list is approved. A further 515 of the 2023 leaving group fail the same
test.

**2. 186 students are paying the University this year but appear on no register.** They are almost
certainly present with their registration uncaptured. This is the clearest follow-up list here.

**3. 776 of the 1,670 Year 1 students have no transaction of any kind.** Year 1 is exempt from the
money test, so none of them is tested. 418 do hold a registration and are likely just unbilled;
the other **358 have neither a transaction nor a registration** and need checking.

## 12. Notes

- Programme length is taken from the programme catalogue, checked against the fee structures
  (which agree for 126 of 131 programmes, including all 40 diplomas) and the curriculum.
- Positive transactions are the higher of the general ledger and the fee-tracking table, never
  the sum — adding them would count 70 students' payments twice.
- Three records need cleaning: a stray Year 4 on the BBA fee structure, 18 courses tagged to a
  Year 3 on two-year diplomas, and 5 live students sitting on a test programme record.
- Registration for 2026/2027 is still open, so these are a position rather than a final census.

*Figures for continuing students on the registration basis, and the Academic Registrar's query on
Year 3, are in the separate report `SENATE_CONTINUING_STUDENTS_2026-2027.md`. That report excludes
final-year students and so reports a smaller number — 2,042 — answering who will still be enrolled
next year rather than who is studying now.*
