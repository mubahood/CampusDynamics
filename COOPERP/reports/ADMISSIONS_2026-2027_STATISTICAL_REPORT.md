## 14. How the numbers were produced

- **Cohort**: `acad_student.entryyear = '2026'`, reconciled against `acad_applications`
  (§2). Every table in this report is drawn from that same fixed list of 1,850, so the
  breakdowns are directly comparable.
- **Registered**: an `acad_registration` row for `acad_year = '2026/2027'` with status
  REGISTERED, LATE REGISTERED or CLEARED. Restricting to this academic year matters — 41
  students also carry 2025/2026 rows, and counting those would overstate registration by 12.
- **Paid**: `GREATEST(ledger credits, tracking payments)`, **not their sum**. The two finance
  sources overlap for 1,030 of the 1,033 payers; adding them would have reported **UGX 1,092.8m**
  against a true **559.5m** — nearly double. Reversals and balance-fix adjustments are excluded.
- Payments include deposits made **before** 1 August 2026 (608 transactions), which is correct:
  fees are paid during the admission window, before the year opens.
- Every breakdown was checked to sum back to 1,850, and every cross-tab to its own margins.
- Money is rounded to 0.1m per line, so the campus column reads 559.4m against a true 559.5m.
  The level column, rounding differently, lands exactly on 559.5m. Neither is an error in the
  underlying figure.
