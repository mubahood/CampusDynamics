# MUTEESA I ROYAL UNIVERSITY
## Report to Senate — Continuing Student Body, Academic Year 2026/2027

**Prepared:** 14 August 2026
**Source:** Campus Dynamics EMIS — semester registrations for 2026/2027, live extract
**Scope:** Students in Years 1, 2 and 3 who are **continuing**. Finalists are excluded.

---

## 1. How the cohort was defined

Senate asked for the students in Years 1, 2 and 3, **not counting the third years who are
about to graduate**. Those two requirements only reconcile if "finalist" is read as *the
student's final year of their own programme* rather than "Year 3" as a flat number — because
Year 3 means opposite things on a three-year and a four-year programme.

So the rule applied throughout this report is:

> A student is a **FINALIST** when their current year of study has reached the length of their
> programme, and is **CONTINUING** otherwise.

That excludes every Year 3 on a three-year degree — the graduands Senate asked us to leave out —
while correctly keeping the Year 3 engineers, who still have a year to run.

|---|---:|
| Registered for 2026/2027 with a year of study | **3,292** |
| *less* finalists — in the final year of their programme | −1,250 |

## 8. Registration still outstanding

Not every continuing student has registered for 2026/2027 yet.

| Group | Kakeeka | Kirumba | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Registered 2025/2026, **not yet** 2026/2027 | 550 | 470 | **1,020** |
| Dormant — no registration in either year | 299 | 299 | **598** |

**1,020 students are the immediate follow-up list.** They were with us last year and have not
returned to the register. Against a current cohort of 2,042 this is a very large proportion, and
until they register the figures in this report understate the true continuing body. The 598
dormant students are a separate question — whether they are on leave, transferred out, or
should be written off the roll.

highest carry-forward — the same students appear in both.

The 820 currently-registered students still flagged ALUMNI is the one finding that needs
attention outside this report: any process that trusts `new_status` — portal access, mailing
lists, graduation lists — will treat those 820 as having left. This report deliberately
determines standing from **semester registration**, which is auditable, rather than from that
flag.

## Summary for Senate

1. **2,042 continuing students** in Years 1–3, after removing 1,250 finalists — of whom 739 are
   the third-year graduands Senate asked to exclude.
2. **The two campuses are comparable in size** (Kirumba 1,126 / Kakeeka 916) **but not in
   composition.** Kirumba is engineering and science; Kakeeka is business, humanities and
   teacher training.
3. **Gender parity overall (50.5% female) is a mix effect.** Education is 69% female, STEAD is
   24% female. The disparity is disciplinary, not institutional.
4. **1,020 students from last year have not yet registered.** This is the largest single number
   in the report and the figures here will not be final until it is resolved.
5. **One student in five carries a failed paper forward; among Kirumba Year 3, more than one in
   four.** Retake capacity should be planned against the per-student figure, not the 96% paper
   pass rate.
6. **64% of the cohort is on a programme taught at both campuses.** The same awards are issued
   from two sites, which argues for common curriculum governance and shared external examination.
