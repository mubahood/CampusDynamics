## Preamble

Staff Performance Appraisal is part of the Performance Management System of Muteesa I Royal University. It is used as a management tool for establishing the extent to which set targets within the overall goals of the University are achieved. Through the staff performance appraisal, performance gaps and development needs of an individual employee are identified. The appraisal process offers an opportunity to the appraisee and appraiser to dialogue and obtain feedback on performance. This calls for a participatory approach to the appraisal process.

The appraiser and appraisee are advised to read the detailed guidelines before filling this form.

## Appraisal Process (Procedure)

The appraisal is conducted by the supervisor(s) designated by the Human Resource Office and approved by Management and the Governing Council, in accordance with the MRU Organizational Chart.

### Steps:

1. Complete an Academic Staff Performance Appraisal Form for each subordinate employee.
2. Schedule a meeting to discuss the appraisal with the appraisee.
3. Conduct the performance appraisal meeting.
4. Provide a copy of signed appraisal form to the appraisee.
5. Send the original copy to the Human Resource Office for the employee's personnel file.

### Rules:

- No change of performance appraisal will be accepted **30 calendar days** after the appraisal deadline.
- The employee has the right to appeal in writing to the Human Resource Office for Management validation within **30 calendar days** of receiving the copy of the signed appraisal form.

## Performance Level Definitions

| Level | Rate | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Exceptional** | 5 | Work performance consistently exceeds competent levels. A high degree of proficiency is shown in most aspects of performance. |
| **Above Expectations** | 4 | Work is fully satisfactory and work performance often exceeds competent levels. |
| **Satisfactory** | 3 | Work performance consistently meets and occasionally may exceed expected levels. Employee meets job requirements. |
| **Development Needed** | 2 | Some performance is below the competent level. Employee needs to improve during the next appraisal period, subject to observations in a Warning Letter. |
| **Unsatisfactory** | 1 | Performance is consistently below competent level. Immediate improvement required. Must demonstrate improvement within a short period (e.g. 3 months) before re-appraisal. If no improvement, a Warning Letter will be issued. |

### Overall Assessment Formula

> Overall Score = Sum of all scores ÷ Total number of outputs
