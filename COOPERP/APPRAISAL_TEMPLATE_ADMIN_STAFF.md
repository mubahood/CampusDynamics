## PREAMBLE

Staff Performance Appraisal is part of the Performance Management System of Muteesa I Royal University. It is used as a management tool for establishing the extent to which set targets within the overall goals of the University are achieved. Through the staff performance appraisal, performance gaps and development needs of an individual employee are identified. The appraisal process offers an opportunity to the appraisee and appraiser to dialogue and obtain a feedback on performance. This therefore, calls for a participatory approach to the appraisal process. The appraiser and appraisee are advised to read the detailed guidelines before filling this form.

## APPRAISAL PROCESS

The appraisal is conducted by the supervisor(s) designated by the Human Resource Office and approved by Management and the Governing Council, in accordance with the MRU Organizational Chart. The supervisor(s) will follow the steps outlined thus:

1. Complete Administrative Staff Performance Appraisal Form for each subordinate Employee
2. Schedule a meeting to discuss the appraisal with the Appraisee
3. Conduct performance appraisal meeting
4. Provide a copy of signed appraisal form to the Appraisee
5. Send the original copy to Human Resource Office for inclusion in the Employee's personnel file
6. No change of performance appraisal will be accepted 30 calendar days after the appraisal deadline
7. The Employee has the right to appeal in writing to Human Resource Office for Management validation within 30 calendar days of receiving the copy of the signed appraisal form
