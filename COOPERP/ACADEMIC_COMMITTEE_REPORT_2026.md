## KEY OBSERVATIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

### Observations

1. **Strong growth trajectory:** Total registered enrolment grew from 721 (2023/2024) to 2,475 (2024/2025) to 2,437 (2025/2026), a 238% increase over three years.

2. **Female majority:** Female students constitute 56.7% of the 2025/2026 cohort (1,381 of 2,437). This pattern is consistent across all three years. Female dominance is most pronounced in the Faculty of Education (67.0% F) and Faculty of Business & Management (67.6% F).

3. **Gender imbalance in STEM:** The Faculty of Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Design is 74.4% male in 2025/2026. Within Engineering specifically, female enrolment is under 17% for both Civil and Electrical Engineering programmes.

4. **Faculty of Education dominates:** The Faculty of Education accounts for 46.3% of all active students (1,128 of 2,437) in 2025/2026, making it by far the largest faculty. The Diploma in Primary Education (204) and Bachelor of Education with ICT (313) are the two highest-enrolled programmes university-wide.

5. **Many registered programmes inactive:** Of 131 registered programmes, 83 (63.4%) have zero currently enrolled active students. This includes the entire Certificate category (26 of 27 programmes) and many legacy Diploma and Bachelors programmes.

6. **Masters programmes are under-enrolled:** Only 32 active students are enrolled across 4 active postgraduate programmes. The MBA has 10 students, MEd Management has 17. Postgraduate expansion may be an area of strategic focus.

7. **Significant growth in STEAD:** The Faculty of Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Design grew from 177 students (2023/2024) to 618 (2025/2026), a 249% increase — the fastest-growing faculty.

### Recommendations to the Committee

1. **NCHE data capture:** A data collection exercise should be undertaken to populate the following fields in the MIS for all 131 programmes: Review Date, Date Submitted to NCHE, NCHE Comments, Accreditation Date, Next Review Date, Date Approved by Council.

2. **Programme rationalisation:** The committee should consider reviewing the 83 inactive programmes to determine whether they should be formally deactivated, archived, or have their accreditation status reviewed with NCHE.

3. **Duplicate codes:** Several programme codes appear to be duplicate entries (e.g., multiple codes for "Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering" under faculty code "—"). These should be cleaned in the MIS.

4. **Gender equity in STEM:** Targeted recruitment and retention initiatives may be warranted to improve female participation in Engineering and technology programmes.

5. **Postgraduate development:** With only 32 postgraduate students university-wide, strategies to grow Masters enrolment should be explored.
